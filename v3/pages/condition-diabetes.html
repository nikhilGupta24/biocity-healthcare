The India Crisis

Know Your Risk

Who should get
tested today?

If any of these apply to you, a blood test isn't optional — it's urgent.

Family History

Parent or sibling with diabetes doubles your risk. Genetics load the gun — lifestyle pulls the trigger.

Overweight / BMI > 25

Excess belly fat causes insulin resistance. Every 5 kg above ideal weight increases risk by 30%.

Age Over 35

Risk spikes after 35. Indian ethnicity means diabetes hits 10 years earlier than global averages.

Gestational Diabetes

Had high sugar during pregnancy? 50% chance of developing Type 2 within 5-10 years.

PCOS / Hypertension

High BP and polycystic ovary syndrome are strongly linked to insulin resistance.

Sedentary Lifestyle

Less than 150 mins/week of activity? Your cells forget how to use insulin efficiently.

Warning Signs

Your body whispers
before it screams.

These symptoms often go ignored for years. Recognize them early.

Excessive Thirst

Drinking water constantly but still parched? High sugar pulls fluid from tissues.

Frequent Urination

Waking up 3-4 times at night? Your kidneys are working overtime to flush excess sugar.

Unexplained Fatigue

Sugar stays in blood instead of fueling cells. Energy crashes despite eating enough.

Blurry Vision

High sugar warps the lens of your eye. Left unchecked, it leads to permanent damage.

Why It Matters

Uncontrolled diabetes
damages silently.

Every year without detection accelerates irreversible organ damage.

Heart Disease

Diabetics are 2-4x more likely to have a heart attack. High sugar damages blood vessel walls silently over years.

Kidney Failure

Diabetes is the #1 cause of kidney failure in India. Microalbumin test catches damage years before symptoms.

Vision Loss

Diabetic retinopathy affects 1 in 3 diabetics. Annual eye screening + sugar control prevents 90% of blindness.

Nerve Damage

Numbness, tingling, burning in feet. 50% of diabetics develop neuropathy. Early HbA1c control is the best prevention.

Cognitive Decline

Uncontrolled diabetes increases Alzheimer's risk by 65%. The brain is highly sensitive to sugar fluctuations.

Dental Problems

Gum disease is 3x more common in diabetics. Persistent bad breath and bleeding gums are early red flags.

FAQ

Diabetes testing
questions.

HbA1c (glycated hemoglobin) measures the percentage of hemoglobin coated with sugar over 2-3 months. Unlike fasting glucose which shows a single moment, HbA1c reveals your long-term sugar control — making it the most reliable diabetes test available.
HbA1c does NOT require fasting — it can be done anytime. However, Fasting Blood Sugar requires 8-12 hours of overnight fasting (water is fine). Our team will guide you when you book.
Annually if you're over 35 or have any risk factors. Every 6 months if you're pre-diabetic (HbA1c 5.7-6.4%) or have family history. Every 3 months if you're a diagnosed diabetic monitoring control.
Yes — 100%. Pre-diabetes (HbA1c 5.7-6.4%) is fully reversible with lifestyle changes: 30 mins daily walking, reducing refined carbs, losing 5-7% body weight. The key is catching it early through regular testing.
Diabetes is diagnosed when: Fasting glucose ≥ 126 mg/dL, OR HbA1c ≥ 6.5%, OR random glucose ≥ 200 mg/dL with symptoms. Two separate test occasions are needed for confirmation.
