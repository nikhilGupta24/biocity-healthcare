What's Included

Everything we
check for you.

A single fasting sample runs through nine complete panels — the same tests premium walk-in labs charge far more for.

Complete Blood Count

  • Haemoglobin, RBC, WBC & platelet counts to screen for anaemia, infection and clotting health.

Lipid Profile

  • Total cholesterol, HDL, LDL & triglycerides to assess your heart and cardiovascular risk.

Liver Function (LFT)

  • SGOT, SGPT, bilirubin & proteins to detect fatty liver, inflammation and liver stress early.

Kidney Function (KFT)

  • Urea, creatinine, uric acid & electrolytes to check how well your kidneys are filtering.

Thyroid (T3/T4/TSH)

  • Complete thyroid panel to catch hypo- or hyperthyroidism affecting energy, weight and mood.

Blood Sugar (Fasting + HbA1c)

  • Fasting glucose plus HbA1c for a 3-month sugar average — the gold standard for diabetes screening.

Iron Studies

  • Serum iron, ferritin & TIBC to identify iron-deficiency anaemia and fatigue at the source.

Vitamin D & B12

  • 25-OH Vitamin D and B12 levels — the two deficiencies behind most tiredness, aches and low mood.

Urine Routine

  • Complete urine examination to flag infections, kidney issues and metabolic markers.
