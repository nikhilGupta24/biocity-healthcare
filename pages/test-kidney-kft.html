What is a Kidney Function Test?

The Kidney Function Test (KFT), also called a renal profile, is a group of blood tests that measures how efficiently your kidneys are removing waste products and maintaining the balance of key minerals and electrolytes in your body.

Because kidney disease is often silent in its early stages, this panel is a valuable check for anyone with diabetes, high blood pressure, swelling, changes in urination, or a family history of kidney problems. Early detection makes conditions far easier to manage. Read more on our blog →

What it measures

  • Blood Urea — a waste product filtered by the kidneys
  • BUN (Blood Urea Nitrogen) — reflects protein waste clearance
  • Serum Creatinine — the key marker of kidney filtration
  • Uric Acid — linked to gout and kidney stone risk
  • Sodium, Potassium & Chloride — essential electrolyte balance
  • Calcium — mineral balance regulated by the kidneys
  • eGFR — the estimated overall filtration rate of your kidneys

How to prepare

  • Overnight fasting is recommended for the most accurate results
  • Stay well hydrated with plain water before your sample
  • Avoid a heavy, high-protein meal the night before
  • Tell us about any medicines or supplements you take regularly
