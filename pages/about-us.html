Our Story

From a single idea to
India's home for health.

Biocity Healthcare was built on a simple frustration — that getting a health checkup meant taking a day off, standing in queues, and still waiting days for reports. Our founder Shubham Jain set out to change that.

We assembled NABL-accredited labs, trained phlebotomists, senior pathologists, and expert dieticians under one roof — then delivered it all to your doorstep. We even pioneered India's first video diet consultation, so guidance doesn't stop at a report.

Two decades on, over 50 lakh tests and 39 lakh families later, that mission is unchanged: honest, accurate, affordable diagnostics — with a human touch.
