Why It Matters

Why preventive
testing works.

The cheapest, easiest time to treat almost anything is before you feel it. Here's what a yearly checkup really buys you.

Silent Diseases

Diabetes, high cholesterol, fatty liver and thyroid disorders often show no symptoms for years — a simple blood panel reveals them long before harm sets in.

Early Detection Saves

Catching a condition early can mean a small diet change instead of a lifetime of medication — and a fraction of the cost of treating it late.

Family History

If diabetes, heart disease or thyroid issues run in your family, routine screening turns inherited risk into something you can actually manage.

Lifestyle Risk

Desk jobs, stress, irregular sleep and eating out add up quietly. Testing shows exactly how your habits are affecting your numbers.

Peace of Mind

Nothing beats a clean report reviewed by a doctor. Know where you stand instead of worrying about what you can't see.

Baseline Tracking

One checkup is a snapshot; yearly checkups tell a story. Trends over time help your doctor spot change before it becomes a diagnosis.

Age-Wise Plans

Checkups by
life stage.

What to test — and how often — changes with age and biology. Find the panel that fits where you are right now.

In Your 20s–30s

Test: CBC, blood sugar, lipid profile, thyroid (TSH), Vitamin D & B12, liver & kidney basics.
Cadence: a baseline checkup once every 1–2 years.

In Your 40s

Test: add HbA1c, full thyroid (T3/T4/TSH), cardiac risk markers, complete lipid & liver panels.
Cadence: once a year.

In Your 50s

Test: add cardiac markers, kidney function (KFT), electrolytes, bone profile and relevant cancer markers.
Cadence: yearly, plus doctor review.

60 & Beyond

Test: comprehensive panel — heart, kidney, liver, bone health, Vitamin D/B12, HbA1c and full blood count.
Cadence: every 6–12 months.

Women's Health

Test: iron studies & haemoglobin, thyroid, Vitamin D, hormone & bone profile, plus age-appropriate cancer markers.
Cadence: once a year.

Men's Health

Test: lipid & cardiac risk, blood sugar/HbA1c, liver & kidney panels, Vitamin D, and PSA where advised.
Cadence: once a year.

Questions

Preventive testing,
answered.

It's a curated set of blood and urine tests done while you feel healthy, designed to catch early signs of common conditions — diabetes, high cholesterol, thyroid disorders, anaemia, vitamin deficiencies, and liver or kidney stress — before symptoms appear.
Healthy adults in their 20s–30s benefit from a baseline every 1–2 years. From 40 onward — or earlier if you have a family history or a chronic condition — an annual checkup is recommended, moving to every 6–12 months after 60.
Most preventive panels include fasting tests like blood sugar and lipid profile, so 8–12 hours of fasting (water is fine) is advised. We'll confirm exactly what's needed when you book and offer early-morning slots to make it easy.
Everyone — but it matters most if you're over 40, have a family history of diabetes, heart disease or thyroid issues, live a sedentary or high-stress lifestyle, or simply haven't been tested in over a year.
