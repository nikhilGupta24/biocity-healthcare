Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026.

Introduction

Biocity Health Care LLP ("Biocity", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website, mobile applications, home sample collection, and diagnostic services. By using our services, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information to deliver accurate diagnostics and a smooth experience:

  • Personal details — your name, age, gender, phone number, email address, and residential address used for booking and home collection.
  • Health and sample data — biological samples, test results, medical history, prescriptions, and any clinical information you share with us or our partner laboratories.
  • Payment information — billing details and transaction records processed securely through our payment partners. We do not store your full card or banking credentials.
  • Device and usage data — IP address, browser type, device identifiers, pages visited, and interactions collected automatically to improve our website and services.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to schedule and complete home sample collection, process and deliver test reports, verify your identity, take payments, provide customer support, share diet and health guidance, send appointment and report notifications, comply with legal and regulatory obligations, and improve the quality and safety of our services.

Sharing & Disclosure

We share your information only where necessary to deliver our services. This may include our partner NABL-accredited laboratories that process your samples, our phlebotomists and consulting doctors, payment processors, logistics partners, and authorities where disclosure is required by law. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data to any third party for marketing or commercial gain.

Data Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational safeguards to protect your information, including encryption of data in transit, restricted role-based access controls, secure servers, and regular reviews of our security practices. While no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, we work continuously to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

Your Rights

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate details, and request deletion of your data where it is no longer required for legal or medical record-keeping. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, keep you signed in, analyse traffic, and improve your experience. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not function correctly without them.

Retention

We retain your personal and health information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, maintain accurate medical records, and comply with applicable legal, tax, and regulatory requirements. When information is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymise it.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for use by adults. Tests for minors must be booked and consented to by a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children without appropriate guardian consent.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out to us at care@biocityhealthcare.com or call +91 8860-606-141. You can also visit our Contact page.

Questions?

Our care team is happy to help with any privacy questions or requests about your data. Reach out and we'll respond promptly.
