The Scale

What your
BMI means.

BMI ranges give a general guide for adults. Your ideal number can vary with age, muscle mass and body type.

CategoryBMI rangeWhat it suggests
UnderweightBelow 18.5May signal under-nutrition — worth a check with a dietician.
Normal18.5 – 24.9A healthy weight range for most adults. Keep it up.
Overweight25 – 29.9A good time to review diet and activity levels.
Obese30 and aboveHigher risk for lifestyle conditions — consider a full checkup.

A screening tool, not a diagnosis

BMI is a quick indicator that doesn't measure body fat, muscle or fitness directly. For a complete picture of your health, a full body checkup looks at the numbers that really matter — blood sugar, cholesterol, vitamins and more.
