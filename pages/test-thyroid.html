What is a Thyroid Profile Test?

The Thyroid Profile measures the three key hormones that reveal how your thyroid gland — the small butterfly-shaped gland in your neck — is functioning. It combines Total T3, Total T4 and an Ultrasensitive TSH to give a complete picture of your metabolic health.

An underactive (hypothyroid) or overactive (hyperthyroid) gland can cause fatigue, unexplained weight change, hair loss, mood swings and irregular periods. This simple blood test helps diagnose these conditions early so they can be managed effectively. Read more on our blog →

What it measures

  • Total T3 (Triiodothyronine) — the active metabolism hormone
  • Total T4 (Thyroxine) — the thyroid hormone reserve
  • TSH (Ultrasensitive) — the master signal from the pituitary that controls the thyroid

How to prepare

  • No fasting is required for this test
  • A morning sample is preferred for the most consistent results
  • If you take thyroid medication, collect the sample before your morning dose
  • Inform us of any biotin or supplement use, as it can affect results
